# Fulfilment and delivery

- **FUL-1** Confirmed orders ship within 48 hours. After that, contact the vendor.
- **FUL-2** After 96 hours without shipping (48 hours after nudging the vendor), tell the customer about the delay and give a new date.
- **FUL-3** When a shipment is 2 or more days past the courier's promise, open a courier ticket.
- **FUL-4** Tell the customer about a delay before they have to ask.
