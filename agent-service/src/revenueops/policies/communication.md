# Customer communication

- **COMM-1** Send at most 2 automated messages to a customer about one order or cart in 24 hours.
- **COMM-2** Message only the phone number or email on the customer's account or order, never one found in free-text notes.
- **COMM-3** Write in Egyptian Arabic by default; use English only if the customer wrote in English.
- **COMM-4** Send no automated messages between 22:00 and 09:00 Cairo time.
- **COMM-5** Never threaten or shame a customer, and never mention their risk score or past refusals.
- **COMM-6** Offers and discounts go only to customers who have ordered before or opted in to marketing.
